Meroo mutuu, meroo pukuuu, my sweet girl

I have tried to write this properly more times than you will ever know. Every version felt too small. You cannot fit a person into paragraphs. You cannot fit what they do to your life into sentences.

So I will just say it plainly.

You are the best thing that has happened to me. Not the loudest thing. Not the most dramatic. The best. The one that quietly rearranged everything and made it better.

I do not know what the next year looks like. I do not know how many calls, how many voice notes, how many nights we fall asleep with the phone still on. I do not know when the distance finally closes. What I do know is that none of that changes how I feel about you. Not the miles. Not the waiting. Not the days where everything feels too heavy.

You are the person I want to tell everything to. The first one I think of. The last one I think of. The one I want to build a boring, ordinary, beautiful life with.

So here is my promise. Not a grand one. Just an honest one.

There is no choosing with you. You are already mine, and I am already yours. On the good days and the quiet ones. When you are bright and laughing and when you are tired and barely forming sentences. I will keep showing up. I will keep listening. I will keep loving you in every way I know how, and then I will learn new ways.

Fifteen months down. A lifetime left.

Happy monthiversary, my pukuuu.
I love you more than yesterday and less than tomorrow.

Yours, always
